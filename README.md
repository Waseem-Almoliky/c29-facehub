# FaceHub for CUBOT C29

A web app (installable PWA) for browsing, recolouring and installing watch faces on the
CUBOT C29 smartwatch (MoYoung Da Fit firmware, Jieli chip, 360×360 "tpls 72" faces),
straight from the phone over **Web Bluetooth**.

Open it in **Chrome on Android**, tap *Connect watch*, pick **C29**. The Da Fit app must not be
connected at the same time (the watch accepts one connection).

- Watch: switch between the 7 built-in faces and the custom slot (8).
- Store: 345 faces from the Da Fit store (`catalog.json` snapshot; files load from MoYoung's CDN).
- My faces: stored on the phone (IndexedDB). Recolour, favourites, import/share `.bin` files.

Unofficial hobby project, not affiliated with MoYoung or CUBOT.
